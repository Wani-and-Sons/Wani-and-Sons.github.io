About Wani & Sons

Walnut craft, made around you.

Based in the heart of downtown Srinagar, Kashmir, a hub of artisans, Wani & Sons creates useful, lasting pieces from walnut wood chosen for its natural character and strength. We ship our products worldwide.

Our work ranges from furniture and keepsake boxes to carved figures, doors and windows, frames, decor, and bookcases. We welcome custom ideas and shape each piece around its purpose, the wood, and the space it will live in.

The Wani & Sons way

Material first. Details always.

Every piece begins with the grain, tone, and natural shape of the wood. We work carefully so the finished object feels useful today and worth keeping for years.
